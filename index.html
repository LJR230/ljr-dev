The pattern

Every operationally complex business has a version of this:

Someone re-types data from one system into another. Every day.
Scheduling lives in a spreadsheet only one person understands.
Status updates happen by phone call, text, and memory.
The weekly report takes a day to assemble from three tools.

None of these look like technology problems. They're process problems, and the right piece of software makes them disappear. Finding that piece is most of the job.

How I work

01

Understand the business

I sit with the people doing the work and learn how things actually run, not how the org chart says they run.

02

Find the bottleneck

One workflow is usually costing far more time than the rest. That's the target.

03

Design the right fix

Sometimes automation, sometimes an integration, sometimes AI. Often simpler than expected.

04

Build and ship it

I write the code, wire the systems together, and put it in your team's hands.

05

Measure the result

Hours saved, errors gone, steps removed. If it didn't improve the operation, it isn't done.
